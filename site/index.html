Four seasons,
one line of code.

Snow in winter, butterflies and blooming flowers in spring, slow sun glints in summer, falling leaves in autumn. A click-through canvas for any website that can pick the season from today's date.

npm i four-seasons-fx

Hemisphere
Background

Click-through

The canvas uses pointer-events: none, so links and buttons keep working.

Calm by default

Turns off for prefers-reduced-motion and pauses in background tabs.

Knows the date

season="auto" follows meteorological seasons for either hemisphere.

SSR-safe

No window access during server rendering. Works in Next.js, Astro, Nuxt and SvelteKit.

Fits phones

Particle counts scale with the viewport; pixel ratio is capped for battery.

Your palette

Pass colors to tint snow, leaves, petals or glints to your brand.
